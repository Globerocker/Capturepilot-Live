# B2G SaaS Competitive Analysis — Complete Market Landscape

**Date:** April 3, 2026
**Prepared for:** CapturePilot 2.0 Strategic Planning

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The B2G (Business-to-Government) SaaS market has **25+ active platforms** spanning four distinct segments: Enterprise Intelligence ($5K-$42K/yr), Mid-Market Tools ($50-$300/mo), Budget/SMB Tools ($0-$50/mo), and AI-Native Newcomers ($99-$300/mo). The market is rapidly consolidating around AI capabilities — every major platform now offers some form of AI matching, proposal assistance, or document analysis.

**Key Market Dynamics (April 2026):**
- AI proposal writing is becoming table stakes — GovDash, Sweetspot, GovSignals, CLEATUS, and SamSearch all offer it
- Enterprise platforms (GovWin, BGOV) face pressure from cheaper AI-native tools
- SLED (State/Local/Education) coverage is a major differentiator — only ~40% of platforms cover it well
- No single platform dominates across discovery + intelligence + proposals + CRM
- CapturePilot's consulting-hybrid model has NO direct competitor

### CapturePilot's Unique Advantages (NO COMPETITOR HAS THESE)

1. **Consulting + SaaS Hybrid Model** — No competitor offers managed consulting services integrated into the platform. GovWin sells research separately; CapturePilot embeds consulting in the portal.

2. **Portal for Managed Clients** — Dedicated consulting client portal with task management, document sharing, and activity logging. No competitor has this.

3. **Price-to-Intelligence Ratio** — If priced at $50-150/mo, CapturePilot would offer AI win strategy + incumbent intel + 140-point scoring at a fraction of GovWin ($12K+) or even EZGovOpps ($2.7K+).

4. **Deterministic Scoring (Not Black-Box AI)** — CapturePilot's 140-point scoring is rules-based and explainable. Most AI competitors use opaque models.
