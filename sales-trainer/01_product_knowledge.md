## 1. What CapturePilot is

CapturePilot is a federal and state/local capture-intelligence platform built for small U.S. contractors who are already trying to win government contracts but don't have a full BD or capture team behind them. You plug in your NAICS codes, certifications, and target states, and it scores 40,000+ federal opportunities daily against your profile — ranking them HOT, WARM, or COLD — plus surfaces state, county, and city bids from 48 states via HigherGov, Bonfire, and OpenGov scrapers. On top of the match feed you get an AI proposal writer, a voice-powered capability statement builder, a competitor intelligence tracker, a teaming partner directory (80K+ SAM-registered entities), a recompete radar, and an optional done-for-you consulting tier where the team handles capture entirely.

The short version: it's a smarter SAM.gov replacement that does the daily scanning for you, tells you which ones are worth your time, and gives you the tools to actually respond.

## 2. The three commercial paths

### Path 1 — Self-service software

The SaaS product at `app.capturepilot.com`. Users sign up, complete a short onboarding (NAICS codes, certifications, target states, company website), and get a scored match feed the same day. Three paid tiers: Light ($39/mo), Pro ($89/mo), Agencies/Consultants (custom). There's also a permanent Free tier for people not ready to pay yet.

This path is fully self-serve — no sales call required. The primary close for most inbound leads.

**When to mention it on a call**: always, but don't make it the whole pitch if the lead is asking about consulting. Software is the entry point; consulting is where the economics get interesting.

### Path 2 — Co-Pilot (managed-light)

**Note for partner/simulator**: The canonical brief labels this "Co-Pilot" as a mid-tier managed service. As of 2026-06-09, this tier isn't publicly listed as a distinct offering on the pricing page — it lives between the software and full consulting, positioned on qualifying calls. Confirm exact scope and hours-per-month with André before quoting. What's consistent across all internal references:

- A human (Americurial team member) does a portion of the capture work for the client — likely Sources Sought responses, competitor research, and pipeline review — rather than handing everything off.
- Client still uses the CapturePilot software dashboard to see their matches and pipeline.
- Retainer-based, less expensive than full Pilot, more involved than software alone.
- Differentiator from Pilot: the client is still doing some of their own work (responding to solicitations, attending industry days, etc.) — Co-Pilot supplements, it doesn't replace.

**Don't quote a specific price** until you've confirmed with André. Position it as: "There's a middle option where we do the research and strategy work and you handle the writing — it's less than full service and gives you a lot of the output without the full retainer."

### Path 3 — Pilot (full-service consulting / done-for-you)

This is Americurial handling the entire federal capture lifecycle for a client. The pitch on americurial.com says it plainly: retainer covers the work, they profit when you win.

**What's included:**
- Dedicated capture team handles all CO communications (email + fax)
- Full proposal writing + review (human writers backed by CapturePilot's AI)
- SAM.gov registration assistance
- Competitor analysis and incumbent research
- Monthly strategy calls
- Pipeline reviews
- SDVOSB/VOSB/HUBZone/8(a) certification application assistance
- Software access equivalent to Pro

**5% success fee on awarded contracts** — this is the skin-in-the-game structure. Retainer is not contingency-only; there's a monthly retainer plus 5% on wins.

**What's NOT included:**
- Legal review of contracts (they need their own attorney for protest situations)
- Past performance writing for contracts the team didn't work on
- DCAA audit prep
- Clearance sponsorship

**Weekly cadence**: capture check-in call, pipeline review against CapturePilot match feed, any active proposal coordination.

**How to book**: HubSpot intro-call CTA (15-min mission briefing at `meetings-na2.hubspot.com/americurial/intro-call`). No self-serve signup for consulting — qualification call is required.

**Pricing**: From $2,500/mo retainer plus 5% success fee. 12-month minimum is typical. [CONFIRM WITH ANDRÉ before quoting exact retainer range — internal docs have this number but it's not publicly listed.]

**Veteran discount**: Veteran-owned businesses get 20% off all Americurial services. This is prominent on the site. If a prospect is SDVOSB/VOSB, mention it.

## 4. Onboarding process per tier

### Free (post-trial fallback)
No manual onboarding. User signs up, completes the self-serve onboarding flow (NAICS codes, certifications, target states, company website), gets their match feed. If they let their trial expire without a card or card fails, they land here automatically. No outreach unless they opt into nurture emails.

### Light and Pro (self-serve)
1. User clicks the Stripe Payment Link or signs up via the app.
2. Card is captured at signup; 14-day trial starts.
3. Onboarding flow walks them through: company name + website, primary NAICS codes (up to 5), certifications, target states, contact info.
4. The platform crawls their website to pre-fill services and differentiators.
5. Match feed is generated — first HOT/WARM results appear same day.
6. Welcome email from Resend (transactional) + HubSpot nurture sequence begins.
7. On day 15, card is charged. They're live paying customers.

**What happens after signup they often ask about**: Their first capability statement can be drafted on day 1 using the AI editor. Their first proposal can be drafted against any opportunity in their feed within 10 minutes of onboarding. Matches refresh daily (SAM.gov ingestion runs at 02:00 UTC).

### Agencies & Consultants
Same initial signup as Pro. After the qualification call, the CSM flips the workspace to multi-client mode, sets up white-label subdomain if needed, and runs an onboarding call. Timeline: 3–5 business days from signed contract to live.

### Consulting / Pilot clients
No self-serve signup. After the intro call and contract signing:
1. Americurial creates a portal account in CapturePilot (admin-provisioned — they skip the self-onboarding flow).
2. Client gets portal access at their own subdomain (if opted in to white-label) or via the standard consulting portal view.
3. First strategy call scheduled within the first week.
4. Americurial team begins the SAM.gov audit, capability statement review, and initial match deep-dive.
5. Capture calendar set for the first 90 days.

## 6. Features that actually exist

### Quick Company Check (Quick Checker)
A free, no-login tool at `app.capturepilot.com/check`. You enter a website URL and it crawls the site, identifies likely NAICS codes, certifications the company probably qualifies for, and generates a preliminary eligibility report. Takes about 60–90 seconds. This is the top-of-funnel tool — roughly 60–70% of new signups have used it first.

When the check runs, a lead brief is pushed to HubSpot automatically (company name, inferred NAICS, readiness score, website).

### Opportunity Match Feed
The core product. 40,000+ federal opportunities scored daily against each user's profile. HOT = 70%+ match score. WARM = 50–69%. COLD = below 50%. Scoring weighs NAICS match (25%), PSC match (15%), set-aside eligibility (20%), geographic fit (15%), contract value fit (15%), and deadline urgency (10%). The feed refreshes daily; new SAM.gov ingest runs at 02:00 UTC.

State/local bids (48 states, SLED) are Pro-only. Sources include HigherGov, Bonfire portals, OpenGov, Socrata, and direct state HTML scrapers.

### AI Proposal Writer
Generates a full proposal section-by-section against any opportunity in the feed. Requires a capability statement to be saved first (it uses that as the company brief). 25 proposals/month on Pro, unlimited on the Agency tier. Pro uses gpt-4o-mini; Agency tier uses GPT-4o. Per-section progress is streamed — you watch it fill in as it writes.

### AI Capability Statement Editor
Voice-powered (Web Speech API), paste-from-transcript, or MP3 upload (Whisper transcription). The editor is TipTap-based with a bubble menu that can improve, shorten, expand, or tighten any selected passage via AI. Saves as both plain text and HTML. Can generate a branded PDF for download (Pro only). Can also upload your own existing PDF or DOCX if you have one.

### Competitor Intelligence
80,000+ SAM.gov registered contractors in the database. Each competitor profile shows: services as name+description cards, leadership with names/titles, NAICS codes with labels, past clients, locations, and a "Likely Bid Targets" section that joins against matching opportunities in the feed. Competitor monitoring cron runs daily.

### Teaming Partner Directory
Search the SAM.gov contractor database by NAICS, state, set-aside certification, and keyword. Also includes a curated Certified Teaming tab with ~800 SBA-certified 8(a)/HUBZone/tribal firms, ranked by NAICS overlap with the user's profile. Partners can be saved to a shortlist.

### Recompete Radar
A background cron monitors for opportunities whose incumbents' contract period-of-performance is ending — these are the recompete windows where you have the best shot at displacing an incumbent. Shows up as flagged matches in the feed.

### Pipeline Manager
Kanban board (drag-and-drop via @dnd-kit) and list view. Stages: discovered, researching, preparing, submitted, awarded, lost, no_bid. Custom stage renaming and reordering supported. Per-pursuit detail page has activity timeline, notes, and auto-generated action checklists. Stage changes log to `pipeline_activity`.

### Year-End Spend Radar
Shows per-agency unobligated-balance forecasts. Flags agencies where hot NAICS codes overlap the user's profile. Useful for finding agencies sitting on end-of-year budget that needs to go out fast.

### Email Digest
Daily email with new HOT and WARM matches. Sent via Resend. Pro and Light users get this.

### SBIR Search
Proxies SBIR.gov for R&D solicitations that don't appear on SAM.gov. Useful for tech-focused contractors.

## 7. Features that don't exist yet

Be honest. If a buyer asks for something on this list, the right answer is "that's not in the platform yet."

- **Full SAM.gov passthrough search** — we search our own 40K+ ingested rows, not the full SAM.gov database of 400K+ in real-time. Light and Pro have a SAM.gov passthrough filter, but it's against our indexed subset, not a live API search of everything.
- **Bulk prospect outreach from within the app** — the Agencies tier has outbound prospect campaigns, but this is mostly email sequencing; there's no LinkedIn or phone automation inside the product.
- **GWACs / contract vehicle management** — we track GSA Schedule and eLibrary but there's no in-app GWAC task order module for building out a GWAC pipeline.
- **AI competitive positioning vs. specific incumbents** — the competitor tracker shows you who has what, but there's no "generate a win-theme strategy against this specific incumbent" feature yet.
- **In-app CO contact outreach** — you can see CO names and emails from SAM.gov opportunity notices, but there's no CRM-style outreach tracking inside CapturePilot itself (that lives in HubSpot for consulting clients).
- **Contract vehicle lookup** — no GSA Advantage or GWACs vehicle lookup from within the app.
- **Team collaboration features beyond seats** — 3 seats on Pro means 3 people can log in, but there's no commenting, task assignment between users, or internal chat.
- **Mobile app** — it's a responsive web app. No native iOS or Android app.
- **DCAA compliance tools** — not a finance or accounting product.

## 9. Tech we're built on

For the curious technical buyer who wants to know if this is a real system or a pretty front-end glued to a spreadsheet:

- **Frontend**: Next.js 16 (React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS), deployed on Vercel Pro
- **Database**: Supabase (Postgres) — 40K+ federal opportunities, 80K+ contractors, 91K contacts, job queue for enrichment work
- **Background workers**: 40 Vercel scheduled crons (at the Pro plan ceiling) plus a Railway Playwright worker that handles Cloudflare-protected portals
- **AI**: OpenAI API (gpt-4o-mini / gpt-4o / Whisper)
- **Payments**: Stripe
- **Email**: Resend (transactional) + HubSpot (marketing)
- **Observability**: Vercel Speed Insights + Analytics, Sentry for error monitoring

The platform has been in production since February 2026 and processes daily ingest runs without manual intervention.

André Schuler built CapturePilot. He's based in Germany and was previously running a federal capture agency (Americurial) that was spending hours every week manually scanning SAM.gov for clients. He built the first version of this to replace that manual process, then realized it was worth selling.

Small businesses — the ones the SBA set-aside program was designed for — mostly can't afford those tools. CapturePilot is priced so a veteran-owned HVAC company with 8 employees can actually afford to use it every day.

**Team size**: small. André runs product and engineering. Americurial has a capture team for the consulting work. Don't promise things like "dedicated account manager included at Pro" unless you're talking about the Agency tier, which does include a CSM.
