# The Free Federal Win Audit — the grand-slam offer

The irresistible front-end offer for cold outreach and the Quick Checker funnel.
Hormozi logic: make the dream outcome obvious, make success feel likely, kill the
time and the effort and the risk. Give away something that feels stupid to say no
to, then earn the paid work on the back of the trust.

## The offer, in one line

"Give us one hour. We'll audit your federal setup in detail, hand you 5 contracts
you can actually bid on right now, and send you our $70 Launch Kit free. If it's
not useful, you've lost nothing."

## Why it beats the current ask

The current cold email asks for a retainer relationship ("we run the bids, you
pay nothing unless you win"). That's a good back-end, but it's a big yes for a
cold contact. The audit is a tiny yes: free, one hour, zero risk, and they walk
away with 5 real opportunities even if they never hire us. That's the easy entry.

## What's in it (the value stack)

Everything below, free, in one hour:

- **A real B2G audit, done with you, not a PDF.** We go through your SAM
  registration, NAICS, set-aside eligibility, capability statement, and past
  performance live.
- **5 contracts you can actually bid on.** Not a generic list. Specific notices
  that fit what you do, with deadlines and why you'd be competitive.
- **A "what's stopping you from winning" readout.** The 3 things between you and
  your first (or next) award.
- **The Federal Launch Kit, free.** The same $70, 60-file kit. Theirs to keep.
- **A SAM + capability-statement quick-fix.** The one or two changes that make the
  biggest difference, done on the call.

Anchor it honestly: a capture consultant charges $200 to $400 an hour and the kit
is $70. They get all of it for nothing.

## Handling "I'm already on SAM.gov"

This is the objection that kills generic pitches, so meet it head on:

"Being registered is the starting line, not the win. Most small businesses that
register never win a contract, because registration doesn't tell you which bids
you can actually take or how to position for them. The audit shows you the 5 you
could win this quarter and the 3 things in the way. Registered is exactly who
this is for."

## Front-end to back-end (how it makes money)

The audit is free on purpose. It builds trust and it qualifies. On the call, the
gap between "here are 5 you could win" and "winning takes capture work" sells the
next step on its own:

- **Co-Pilot Program, $1,500/mo** for the founder who wants to run it themselves
  with us in their corner.
- **Custom capture retainer** (success fee, you only pay when you win) for the
  firm that wants it done for them.
- **CapturePilot Pro, $89/mo** for the one who just wants the software.
- For an unsexy-website prospect, the productized **startup package** (website
  refresh + capability statement + Launch Kit + 2 hours consulting + SAM help) as
  a paid one-shot.

We never pitch those in the cold email. We earn the right to pitch them by
delivering the free audit first.

## Risk reversal + scarcity (real, not fake)

- **Guarantee:** "If the hour isn't worth your time, tell us and we'll send you a
  second opportunity list anyway. You keep the kit either way."
- **Honest scarcity:** we can only run a handful of these audits a week by hand,
  so it's first come. Use a real cap (e.g. "we take 5 audits a week"), not a fake
  countdown. The cap is true because the audit is done by a person.

- **Variant A (Sergio's):** 3 matches + "we run the bids, you pay nothing unless
