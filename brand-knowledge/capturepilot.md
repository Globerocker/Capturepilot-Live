# CapturePilot — Brand & Product Knowledge

The federal contract intelligence SaaS. Lives at capturepilot.com (marketing)
and app.capturepilot.com (the product). Built and owned by Americurial LLC.
Founder: Andre Schuler. Public since 2025.

---

## 1. What it is

CapturePilot matches a small business with the federal contracts it can actually
win. It scans 40,000+ federal opportunities daily, scores each one against your
company profile with a proprietary multi-factor engine, and adds AI proposal
drafting, a capability-statement builder, market intelligence, teaming-partner
search, and a deal pipeline. It replaces manual SAM.gov searching.

**Primary tagline:** "Win Government Contracts. Faster."

**Hero subhead (verbatim):** "CapturePilot scans 40,000+ federal opportunities
daily and matches them to your business with our proprietary matching engine.
Stop searching SAM.gov manually. Start winning contracts."

**Positioning line:** "The only platform built from the ground up for small
businesses. Not enterprise tools retrofitted for SMBs."

## 2. Who it's for

Veteran-owned and small businesses chasing federal work: SDVOSB, VOSB, WOSB,
8(a), HUBZone, and tribal/native-owned firms. The sweet-spot customer is a
veteran or 8(a)-eligible firm with 50 or fewer employees and one to five prior
federal awards. Not green enough to need hand-holding on what a NAICS code is,
not big enough to have an in-house capture team.

The reader is a middle-aged operator who's been pitched by twenty competitors
and is allergic to marketing fluff. Write to that person.

## 3. Feature set

**Matching & scoring**
- Proprietary multi-factor scoring engine, rebuilt every 24 hours. Factors:
  NAICS/industry match, set-aside and certification alignment, geography,
  contract value fit, past-performance relevance, deadline urgency.
- Classification: HOT, WARM, COLD. Per-user scoring with save and dismiss.
- An intrinsic opportunity score (0 to 100) independent of any profile. Sources
  Sought and RFIs rank highest because they're the earliest signal.
- Set-aside eligibility gate so a buyer never sees a vendor who can't bid.

**Opportunities**
- 37,000+ active federal opportunities with lifecycle status (active, expiring
  soon, market research, awarded, expired). State, county, and city (SLED)
  opportunities on Pro across 48 states.
- Filter by classification, notice type, set-aside, state, NAICS prefix, security
  clearance, certification. Card, list, and table views. Bulk export to XLSX.
- AI natural-language filter ("show me janitorial sources-sought in Texas").
- Inline PDF preview of attachments, plus attachment OCR and analysis.

**Quick Company Check (free lead magnet)**
- Enter any company website and get a 30-second federal-readiness report: it
  crawls the site, identifies likely NAICS codes, checks SAM.gov registration,
  previews matching opportunities, and surfaces nearby competitors.

**AI proposals**
- AI Proposal Writer reads the full solicitation and drafts a cover letter,
  technical approach, and past-performance structure. First draft in about 15
  minutes. Exports to Word, PDF, or copy-paste. Requires a capability statement.
- Capture Brief: a one-click two-page brief per opportunity with a PWin read,
  bid/no-bid call, and recommended next steps.
- Voice Briefs: hold to record, get a roughly 150-word phone-ready brief.

**Capability statement**
- Rich-text editor with AI editing (improve, shorten, expand, tighten). Talk it
  out loud and the AI writes it. Brand colors auto-pulled from your website.
  Download as a branded PDF, or upload your own. Save to profile and Google Drive.

**Pipeline**
- Kanban (drag-drop) and list views. Stages from discovered through awarded or
  lost. Custom stage names. Activity timeline, notes, action-item checklists.

**Partners & teaming**
- Partner search across 1M+ SAM-registered companies by NAICS, certs, location,
  revenue. A curated directory of about 800 SBA-certified 8(a)/HUBZone/WOSB/
  SDVOSB/tribal firms. 900+ Small Business Liaison Officer contacts at top primes
  (Lockheed, Raytheon, BAE, GDIT, Leidos) for subcontracting outreach.

**Competitors & market intelligence**
- Competitor profiles from 80,000+ SAM contractors: overlap score, federal
  presence, revenue, services, leadership, past clients, likely bid targets.
- Market intelligence: who wins in your industry, total spend, top agencies,
  year-over-year trends. Year-End Spend Radar, Recompete Radar (contracts
  expiring in 3 to 18 months), agency forecast change-detection, IDIQ tracking.

**Integrations:** Claude Desktop (MCP), Zapier, Slack, API + webhooks (Pro),
HubSpot, Resend, Stripe.

**Data sources:** SAM.gov, USASpending.gov, SBIR.gov, SBA Certify, FPDS, Monthly
Treasury balances, GSA eBuy, agency OSDBU forecasts.

## 5. Colors

Emerald is the signal color, sitting on a warm "stone" neutral scale (warm gray,
not cool). The marketing site alternates near-black sections with white.

**Emerald (brand / accent)**
- `#059669` emerald-600 — primary brand green (gradient text, primary buttons, eyebrows)
- `#10b981` emerald-500 — main CTA fill, focus ring, text selection
- `#34d399` emerald-400 — light accent / highlighted hero word
- `#047857` emerald-700 — hover / darker accent
- `#6ee7b7` emerald-300, `#a7f3d0` emerald-200, `#ecfdf5` emerald-50 — tints

**Stone (neutrals)**
- `#1c1917` stone-900 — default body text
- `#0c0a09` — brand black
- `#78716c` stone-500, `#a8a29e` stone-400 — muted text
- `#e7e5e4` stone-200 — borders and the dot-grid pattern (the most-used neutral)
- `#fafaf9` stone-50 — light section background
- `#ffffff` white — page background

**Signature gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #059669 0%, #0ea5e9 100%)` —
emerald into sky-blue (`#0ea5e9`). Used on emphasized headline words.

**Status colors (match scoring)**
- HOT = red `#ef4444` (very high) or amber `#f59e0b` (high)
- WARM = blue `#3b82f6`
- COLD = stone gray
- Success = emerald, warning = amber `#f59e0b`, danger = red, info = blue.

## 9. Voice

The product voice. A vet-owned IT-firm CEO explaining something to his cousin
over coffee. Plain, specific, slightly weary expertise. The reader spots LLM copy
in three seconds and stops trusting it, so don't write LLM copy.

Follow the full house style in [README.md](README.md). The CapturePilot-specific
emphasis: lead with the time saved and the bid not missed, name the real federal
mechanics (NAICS, set-asides, sources sought, sole-source thresholds), and stay
honest about timelines. Federal contracting is slow and the reader knows it;
pretending otherwise loses them.

**Example social post (the right voice):**

> Most small firms find a solicitation about a week before it's due. By then the
> incumbent has known for months. CapturePilot flags the Sources Sought notice
> when it posts, usually six to twelve months out, and tells you whether your
> NAICS and set-aside even qualify. That's the window where you can still win.

**The same idea, wrong (don't do this):**

> 🚀 Unlock your government contracting potential! Our AI-powered platform
> leverages cutting-edge technology to streamline your bid workflow and supercharge
> your win rate. Stop wasting time. Start winning. The future of GovCon is here.

(Em-dashes, buzzword stack, fake hype, no real mechanics, no honesty. Everything
the house style cuts.)
