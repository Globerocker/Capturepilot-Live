# Americurial — Brand & Product Knowledge

The veteran-owned agency that builds CapturePilot. Lives at americurial.com.
Legal entity: Americurial LLC, a Delaware C-Corp based in Phoenix, AZ. Founded
2024 by two combat veterans. Veteran-owned small business.

## 1. What it is

Americurial is a veteran-owned digital agency with two sides that reinforce each
other: a federal-contracting (GovCon) capture agency for veteran-owned firms, and
a software studio (SaaS, GovTech, web, AI). It's the agency that built
CapturePilot and uses the same platform to run capture for its clients. That's
the differentiator: the only agency with its own SaaS platform.

## 3. The model (how Americurial makes money, and why it's the pitch)

- **Retainer covers the work, a 5% success fee aligns the incentive.** "If you
  don't win, we don't profit." This is framed as the business model, not a feature.
- **20% lifetime veteran discount** on everything, verified by DD-214 or VA letter.
- **Two ways in (current pricing):**
  - **Co-Pilot Program, $1,500/mo** — transparent and directly bookable. Coaching
    plus reviews, your team runs the pursuits. 3-month minimum, then month-to-month.
    A 3-hour deep-dive workshop every 6 months. CapturePilot Pro included. No success fee.
  - **Custom Capture Retainer** — no published price, scoped on a call by size,
    NAICS codes, locations, and pursuit volume. Full done-for-you, 5% success fee,
    free website refresh included.
  - Fixed-scope one-time services (SAM Launch Kit, single proposal, capability
    statement, certification support, Sources Sought sprint) still carry published prices.
  - We publish what we honestly can; the full retainer is scoped to the actual work
    instead of a rate card.

The market framing: roughly $700B in federal spend, about 3% (around $25B/yr)
set aside for SDVOSBs, 40,000+ opportunities tracked, and the blunt truth that
most veteran firms never claim their share.

## 5. Colors

Dark-first. Emerald is the signal, amber is the veteran/discount accent, with a
patriotic red-white-blue "honor stripe" reserved for veteran content.

**Surfaces**
- `#0c0a09` stone-950 — background (near-black, the default canvas)
- `#e7e5e4` stone-200 — foreground / body text (off-white)
- `#1c1917` stone-900, `#44403c` stone-700 — dark surfaces, borders
- `#d6d3d1` stone-300, `#a8a29e` stone-400 — muted text

**Emerald (primary)**
- `#10b981` emerald-500 — primary brand, CTAs, links, selection, glow
- `#34d399` emerald-400, `#6ee7b7` emerald-300, `#a7f3d0` — gradient ramp / accents

**Amber (secondary, veteran / discount)**
- `#f59e0b` amber-500, `#fbbf24` amber-400, `#fde68a` — gradient ramp

**Honor stripe (veteran content only)**
- red `#b91c1c`, white `#e7e5e4`, blue `#1e3a8a` — a 2px red-white-blue accent

**Gradients**
- emerald: `linear-gradient(135deg, #10b981 0%, #34d399 50%, #6ee7b7 100%)`
- amber: `linear-gradient(135deg, #f59e0b, #fbbf24, #fde68a)` (veteran emphasis)

**Status (UI demos):** HOT = red, WARM = amber.

**One light-theme exception:** the `/services/govcon` page inverts to a white
background with dark text and emerald-600/700 accents. Everything else is dark.

## 6. Typography

- **Font: Inter** (Google font, loaded via next/font). Monospace
  (`ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular`) only for small UI accents like keyboard pills.
- **Headings are very heavy and very tight:** font-black (900), `tracking-tighter`,
  tight leading (around 0.9), white, scaling up to roughly 5rem on large screens.
- **Eyebrows / labels:** tiny, uppercase, very wide tracking (about 0.3em), emerald.
- **Body:** stone-300/400, relaxed leading. Numbers use tabular figures.

## 8. Visual style

A dark "tactical / mission-ops" aesthetic with emerald as the signature. Sober
and B2B-credible, not gradient-heavy AI styling.

- **Backgrounds:** faint emerald dot-grid and line-grid. Large blurred floating
  orbs in emerald and amber. A subtle noise overlay for a premium, non-flat feel.
- **Glow:** emerald box-shadow glow on primary CTAs, solid emerald text selection.
- **Cards:** translucent dark (`stone-900/40` to `/70`) with backdrop blur, faint
  white borders that turn emerald on hover. Large radii (`rounded-2xl`/`3xl`),
  pill CTAs. Hover-lift and cursor-parallax tilt.
- **Signature motifs:** radar rings behind the hero, a "Live Opportunity Radar"
  feed card, a LIVE status pill, the red-white-blue honor stripe on veteran
  content, animated counters, an animated mission-trace SVG line.
- **Motion:** staggered fade-in-up, scale-in, floating orbs, shimmer, a reading
  progress bar, a blinking typewriter caret.

## 9. Voice

Confident, blunt, plain-spoken US English with a military operator's cadence.
B2B-credible and sober, but punchy and anti-jargon. It leans on mission
vocabulary (recon, position, capture, win; first contact; line of departure) and
on aligned-incentive, no-BS framing (skin in the game, no win no fee, execution
over theater). It's a touch more irreverent than CapturePilot, and it can say
"we" with authority because the people writing it actually served.

Follow the full house style in [README.md](README.md). The Americurial-specific
emphasis: be accountable and concrete about the model (the 5% success fee, the
20% veteran discount, fixed pricing), and use the military framing without
cosplaying. It should read like an operator who ran missions and now runs
pursuits, not like a brand borrowing camo for flavor.

Representative lines from the site (the right voice):

> "We don't just write proposals after the RFP drops. We position you 6 months
> earlier, find the right teaming partners, and close."

> "5% success fee isn't a feature. It's the business model. If you don't win, we
> don't profit."

> "OpenAI in production, not in a demo notebook."

> "15 minutes on the phone. If we're not right for you, we'll tell you, and
> probably recommend someone who is."

**Example social post (the right voice):**

> The government sets aside about $25B a year for veteran-owned firms. Most of it
> goes unclaimed because nobody told these owners how the pipeline works. We're
> two combat vets who learned it the hard way and built the software to run it.
> Retainer covers the work. We only profit when you win. That's the whole pitch.

**Wrong (don't do this):**

> 🎖️ Empowering veteran entrepreneurs to unlock their full potential! Our
> best-in-class, AI-powered capture solutions leverage cutting-edge technology to
> transform your GovCon journey. Let's revolutionize your win rate together!

(Em-dashes, emoji-hype, buzzword stack, no real numbers, no accountability. Cut
all of it.)
