# Loom scripts — lifecycle outreach (record for tomorrow)

Same voice as the match-drop set: open by naming the skepticism, "we didn't guess —
we ran your data through our software," one specific hook, the offer (we run the
bids, you pay nothing unless you win), low-pressure close, dry and human. ~45–60s each.
Generic on purpose — record once, send to everyone in that segment.

---

## 1. SAM registration about to expire  (segment: expiring_registration)

Okay, real quick, because you've got better things to do than another video from
a stranger. This isn't a guess — we pulled your SAM registration before I hit
record, and yours is set to expire soon. Here's why that's not a "deal with it
later" thing: the day it lapses, you don't just go quiet, you go invisible. A
contracting officer literally can't award to you, and you fall out of every search
a buyer runs. I've watched a firm lose a live bid over a registration that expired
by four days. While we were in there, we also pulled a handful of open contracts
that actually fit what you do — they're in the email below. The offer's simple and
a little ridiculous: we run the bids, you pay us nothing unless you win. So renew,
and then have something worth renewing for. Reply if you want the list. If not, no
hard feelings — but maybe renew anyway.

---

## 2. Freshly registered on SAM  (segment: new_registrant)

So you just finished your SAM registration. Genuinely, congrats — most people give
up somewhere in the middle of that. Now the part nobody mentions: registering
doesn't make the phone ring. SAM is the front door, not the invitation. We ran your
new registration through our software, and the codes you're set up under line up
with a bunch of live opportunities right now — they're in the email under this.
What most new registrants do is sit and wait to get found. That's not how it works;
you have to go at the bids. Which is the whole reason I'm here: we run them for you,
you pay nothing unless you actually win. No retainer, no "onboarding package." Reply
and I'll send the matches and walk you through one. If not, all good — you already
did the hard part.

---

## 3. Haven't bid in ~2 years (dormant)  (segment: dormant_2yr)

Alright, short version, because I'm guessing federal work slid to the back burner a
while ago. We pulled your record, and it looks like you haven't put in a bid in
about two years. No judgment — usually it just means the last one was a paperwork
nightmare and nobody had the time to go again. But here's the thing most people miss:
your past performance and your registration are still good, which puts you in a
better spot to win than a brand-new firm starting from zero. We ran your profile and
found a few open contracts that fit what you've already done — in the email below.
We run the bids, you pay nothing unless you win, so getting back in costs you a reply
and not much else. Worth a look? If not, delete away — it's not like I rehearsed this.
