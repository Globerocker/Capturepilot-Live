# B2G Consulting Agency Competitive Landscape — Americurial Market Intelligence

**Date**: 2026-04-17
**Prepared for**: Americurial / CapturePilot Strategic Planning
**Scope**: Federal government contracting (GovCon) consulting agencies across three size tiers — parallel to the software competitor analysis in `COMPETITIVE_ANALYSIS.md`.

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The GovCon consulting market is fragmented across **three distinct tiers** with very different economics:

- **Large ($10M+ revenue, 50+ employees)**: Methodology-owning firms like Shipley and Lohfeld; increasingly PE-rolled-up; few publish pricing
- **Medium ($2M–$10M, 10–50 employees)**: Niche specialists (Winvale = GSA, Redstone GCI = DCAA, GDI = OASIS+); highly demo-gated
- **Small / Boutique (<$2M, 1–10 people)**: Founder-led personal brands (Neil McDonnell, Eric Coffie, Josh Frank); free community → paid courses; often stronger content engines than the large firms

**Key findings for Americurial positioning**:

1. **Published pricing is vanishingly rare.** Only **3 of 20+ agencies publish clear prices**: RSM Federal ($199–$249/mo), Shipley ($4,995 training bundle), OST ($795–$2,995 courses). Transparency alone is a differentiator.
2. **PE rollup is consolidating the large tier**: Shipley + Lohfeld (Petra/Patriot), AOC Key Solutions (→Tyto Athene, Nov 2024), SM&A (→Odyssey), Left Brain Pro (→Stambaugh Ness, Apr 2024). Independent alternatives have room.
3. **AI positioning is claimed everywhere, differentiated nowhere** — Shipley, Lohfeld, Hinz, ProposalHelper all say "AI" or "HITL" but none publish measurable output metrics. Americurial bundled with CapturePilot SaaS can leapfrog.
4. **Niche specialization wins small**: Winvale (GSA), Redstone GCI (DCAA), Koprince (SBA law), GovCon Chamber (daily LinkedIn Live), GovCon Giants (YouTube). Pick a wedge.
5. **TargetGov is closing in 2026** (Gloria Larkin, health) — an SMB/8(a) client book is becoming available.
