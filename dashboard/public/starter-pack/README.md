## How to use the spreadsheets

Every XLSX has a hidden **Lists** tab (don't unhide it — that's where the dropdown options live). When you click a yellow cell, you'll see a dropdown arrow on the right edge — click it to pick from valid options. This works in:

- ✅ Microsoft Excel (Windows + Mac)
- ✅ Apple Numbers
- ✅ Google Sheets (upload the .xlsx to Drive — dropdowns survive the conversion)

If you don't see dropdown arrows in Google Sheets: refresh the tab. Sometimes Sheets takes a moment to register the Lists references after import.

## How to use the Word docs

Fields wrapped in `{{DOUBLE_CURLY_BRACES}}` are placeholders — replace with your information. Word's Find & Replace (Ctrl/Cmd-H) is fastest. Open in Word, Apple Pages, or Google Docs — they all preserve the structure.
